Problem: New Students often do not know what clubs are there in DLSL and do not know where to check where the active clubs are or the recruitment periods and requirements to join that club. And sometimes they still need to ask what clubs there are at the freedom wall.

Purpose: The purpose of this project is to remove the confusion between new students when trying to find their interested club and make sure that they have a website to look for announcements, requirements and the recruitment period.

Overview: This project features a searchable directory for the student clubs where they can search what clubs to join and also shows the requirements and recruitment info and also allow students to ask questions on that specific club on that website.

Scope: 
Only intended for DLSL Students, Teachers, and Alumni who want to know the information about active clubs. 
 Includes all searchable clubs with their specific status.
Also have announcements hubs for application schedules and club related announcements. 
Will be able to create  accounts to login or sign up in the website
The will have the notifications on their gmail and sync on their DLSL gmail
clubs. 
Events and attendance tracking for clubs are included.
Will have super admin, club advisers and club officers for maintaining the clubs

Limitations:
 
Students cannot apply on clubs on this website only information on the schedule and club related announcements
Cannot post any pictures for the clubs. 
Only students and teachers with DLSL emails can access the website.
